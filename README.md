📚 SmartStudy – Study Planner App
SmartStudy is an Android-based study planner that allows students to organize their study activities and schedules in a structured manner.  
It is designed to help users manage time efficiently and maintain a consistent study routine.

🎯 Purpose of the App
Many students struggle with managing multiple subjects and deadlines.
SmartStudy helps users:
    Plan study tasks clearly
    Allocate time effectively
    Stay consistent with their study routine
    
Key Features
  📝 Create and manage study tasks
  📆 Organize daily and weekly study plans
  ⏱️ Assign time durations for subjects
  📚 Manage multiple subjects easily
  📱 Simple and student-friendly UI    

🛠️ Technologies Used
  Language: Kotlin
  Platform: Android
  IDE: Android Studio
  Build Tool: Gradle
